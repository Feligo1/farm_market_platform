Before buying or selling

Five practical checks

Useful whether you are trading poultry, cattle, goats, fish or animal products.

  1. Check who you are trading with. Confirm their name, contact number and location. An admin-reviewed listing is not a health or identity certificate.
  2. Agree on measurement and condition. Specify animal count, live weight if relevant, age or grade, vaccination records where applicable, or fish quality and quantity.
  3. Verify the current movement rules. For live animals, check official veterinary restrictions, movement permits and documentation before travelling.
  4. Calculate actual costs. Include feed, transport, veterinary costs, handling, storage and expected losses before deciding a selling price.
  5. Confirm payment and collection safely. Verify buyers independently; do not pay strangers an advance fee to secure a promised transaction.

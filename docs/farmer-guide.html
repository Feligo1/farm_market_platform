Before planting

Check the actual rainfall outlook

Don't assume one heavy downpour means the rainy season has become established in your district.

  • Read official daily and weekly forecasts for your location.
  • Follow district-specific agrometeorological bulletins before planting.
  • Consult an agricultural extension officer when conditions are uncertain.

After harvesting

Protect your stored grain

Moisture, insects and poor storage conditions can reduce both grain quality and the price you receive.

  • Dry grain adequately before storing it.
  • Keep grain and sacks away from dirty or wet surfaces.
  • Clean and ventilate storage areas; inspect stored crops regularly.
  • Do not mix mouldy or damaged grains into sound grain.

Before selling

Know your real selling costs

A higher price in another town does not necessarily mean you will make more money after paying for transport and storage.

  • Compare published reference prices with real buyer quotations.
  • Calculate production, transport and storage expenses.
  • Confirm buyer identity and payment arrangements before delivering produce.
